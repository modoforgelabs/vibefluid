Questions

Will a live wallpaper drain my battery?

VibeFluid draws frames only while the wallpaper is visible and stops entirely when nothing is moving. Battery saver mode, on by default, also caps frame rates and renders effects at a lighter resolution. You can switch to Performance mode in Settings if you prefer maximum smoothness.

Why does the Music Visualiser ask for microphone access?

Android requires that permission for any app that reacts to the sound your phone plays. VibeFluid reads your phone's audio output, never the microphone, and nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. If you'd rather not grant it, Random mode dances to its own beat with no access needed.

What is location used for?

Only Starfield and Live Weather Sky use it, to draw your sky and fetch your weather. It is optional, rounded to about 11 km and kept on your phone; without it the app uses your time zone. You can delete it any time in Settings.

Which phones does it work on?

Any phone running Android 8 or later. Some effects use Android 13's graphics features and fall back to a simpler look on older versions.

I bought a wallpaper on another phone. How do I get it back?

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account. Purchases are tied to your account, not your device.
